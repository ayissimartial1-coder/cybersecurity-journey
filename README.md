**Status:** ✅ Completed

## Overview
This room covers the human side of cybersecurity: the everyday practices that protect individuals and organisations from common threats.

## What I Learned

### Common Attacks
- Phishing and social engineering
- Malware and ransomware
- How attackers exploit human behaviour and how to recognise warning signs

### Backup Processes
- Why regular backups are critical for recovery
- The 3-2-1 backup strategy (3 copies, 2 media types, 1 offsite)

### MFA & Password Management
- Using strong, unique passwords for every account
- Benefits of password managers
- How multi-factor authentication adds a layer of protection even if credentials leak

### Public Network Safety
- Risks of unsecured public Wi-Fi (e.g., eavesdropping, rogue access points)
- Using VPNs and avoiding sensitive activity on untrusted networks

### Updates & Patches
- How unpatched software leads to exploitable vulnerabilities
- Enabling automatic updates and applying patches promptly

## Key Takeaway
Security isn't just technical. Good habits and awareness are often the strongest defense.
